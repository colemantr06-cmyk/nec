Solaire means "of the sun." Designed to celebrate sunshine and space, this 58-foot Two Oceans power catamaran delivers an exceptional on-water experience centered around expansive outdoor living and seamless comfort.

At anchor, Solaire becomes a true floating playground — crystal-clear waters, vibrant reefs, a FunAir inflatable slide, Bote 360 floating bar, and endless open sky.

Your Team

Solaire Crew

Captain
Sam Crone
New Zealand · RYA Yachtmaster
Sam grew up in New Zealand, spending his childhood sailing and diving, which fostered a lifelong connection to the ocean. He served as a Naval Officer in the Royal New Zealand Navy for 10 years, specializing in navigation. A PADI Divemaster who has explored waters throughout Indonesia, the Caribbean, and the Pacific — and a certified sailing instructor — he loves sharing the beauty of the ocean both above and below the surface. Now in his fourth year as a charter captain, Sam is dedicated to creating lifelong memories on the water.
RYA Yachtmaster Offshore · PADI Divemaster · STCW
Chef / Stew
Matilda Kopra
Finnish · Quarterdeck Trained
A private chef with ten years of experience in the food and hospitality industry. Matilda's career has taken her across Europe — from private chalets in the French and Swiss Alps to yachts along the Mediterranean. Her culinary approach is rooted in wholesome, health-forward cuisine that's vibrant, balanced, and deeply satisfying. Mediterranean flavors are especially close to her heart, and she always tailors every meal with care and attention to the tastes and needs of each guest.
Quarterdeck Academy · STCW · Level 2 Food Safety

The Chef

Matilda
Kopra

A private chef with ten years of experience in the food and hospitality industry. Matilda's career has taken her across Europe — from private chalets in the French and Swiss Alps to yachts along the Mediterranean, where she has had the pleasure of cooking for guests in some of the most beautiful settings in the world.

Her culinary approach is rooted in creating food that not only tastes great but also makes you feel good — wholesome, health-forward cuisine that's vibrant, balanced, and deeply satisfying. Mediterranean flavors are especially close to her heart. Matilda is happy to cater to any dietary preferences or restrictions, and always tailors every experience with care and attention to detail — making food a highlight of your time away.
